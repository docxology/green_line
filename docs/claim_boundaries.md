# Claim boundaries

This document is the register of record for what I claim. I write in the
first person and underclaim.

## What I claim

- I describe declaration coverage of self-declared tags and growth-marker
  labels at a stated review date. That is all a read establishes.
- I counter-signal lexical drift toward certification, credential, resume,
  and virtue language, and I stage such observations aside with notes.
  I apply the same substring rule to ordinary and dated labels. This can
  exclude labels such as `masterclass_practice`; it is not intent detection.
  Tags are matching declarations and are normalized separately.
- I archive typed declarations and evaluation context for deterministic replay.
  Hashes identify bytes and declared context, not truth, authorship or custody.
- I can record my next-review question and practice step without upgrading
  marker coverage. Missing review dates stay missing.
- I attribute optional marker references and practice episodes as declarations.
  Combining episodes is an explicit replay policy, not verified coherence.
- I compare pinned declarations only for the same declared subject and policy:
  added, removed, refreshed and still-missing markers. I infer no skill change.

## What I do not claim

- I do not claim competence, mastery, skill, or employability about any
  person. STAGED is not a certification.
- I do not verify that a declared marker exists or supports what it names.
- I do not authorize anything. No status is permission.
- I do not rank, merge, or evaluate the other line instruments. Envelopes I
  export are witness records; their `native_status` values are not comparable
  across lines.
- I do not claim that my registry is the right registry. It is content, and
  a different registry is a different instrument.
- I do not claim my counter-signal detection catches intent. It catches
  vocabulary.
- I do not infer decline from a missing report or compare people. I supply no
  score. Green never compensates for Red refusal, replaces Violet consent,
  makes White absence a defect, or aggregates with Black craft or Golden aspiration.
