# Limits

- A read describes marker declarations, not practice: a declared marker is
  not verified evidence that a mentor met, a drill ran, or a skill grew.
- Applicability is tag intersection [@def:applicability] over the reviewed vocabulary [@def:tag-vocabulary].
- The registry is content, not judgment: record choice encodes what this
  instrument watches for, and another registry is a different instrument.
- Counter-signal detection is lexical; a resume-shaped growth record phrased
  innocently passes intake. The instrument surfaces vocabulary drift, not
  intent.
- The line makes no claim about other lines. Envelopes carrying
  `native_status` values are witness records and are never compared across
  lines.
- Underclaiming is the register of record: docs/claim_boundaries.md is the
  authoritative statement of what the instrument does and does not claim.
- A matching report pointer is not envelope validation. Structural identity,
  declared provenance equality and native report equality are separate checks.
  Hashes do not verify sources, guarantee persistence or authenticate authorship.
- Green remains stageless and never compensates for Red refusal, substitutes
  for Violet consent, makes White absence a defect, or aggregates with Black
  craft or Golden aspiration into a score.
