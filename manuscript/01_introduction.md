# Introduction

## Question

Green Line answers one question: *what is still growing?*

## Job

Its job is capacity under development: apprenticeships, skills deliberately
not yet mastered, with marker/counter-signal staging. Where Black Line
describes how strong work is done and Red Line describes what must never be
crossed, Green Line holds the honest middle: the named, dated, inspectable
record of what someone is still learning on purpose.

## What it is never

The line has one inviolable boundary. Green Line must never become:

- a resume — a list of attained credentials;
- a competence certification — a claim that a skill is mastered;
- a virtue signal — a performance of learning for status.

The counter-signal intake stages observations phrased as certification or
credential language aside with a note rather than scoring them. Growth
records that drift toward those genres are visible as drift, not scored.
